Out of Stock Forecast Dashboard

A supervised learning demand risk model rendered as an operational dashboard: feature importance, a confusion matrix, and a ranked SKU risk list, the shape planners actually use to prioritize replenishment. Compares two model candidates and shows why the deployed model was chosen on recall rather than raw accuracy. Model metrics are illustrative, generalized from a 5 market retail out of stock classification project, not a real company's production numbers.
